# StegSim Roadmap
## BCAT-GCAT-Engine / StegSim

Version: 1.0  
Status: Forward-Looking Architecture Roadmap  
Scope: v1 → v5+

---

# Purpose

This document defines the planned development trajectory for:

```text
BCAT-GCAT-Engine/StegSim
```

StegSim is intended to become the canonical simulation environment for:

- BCAT / GCAT admissibility research
- governance-state simulation
- recoverability analysis
- mutation-aware ecosystems
- trust continuity modeling
- distributed authority validation
- transition-boundary experimentation
- ecosystem-scale replay and reconstruction

The roadmap intentionally prioritizes:

1. mathematical clarity
2. deterministic replay
3. explainable governance behavior
4. measurable admissibility geometry
5. ecosystem recoverability

over:

- flashy AI demonstrations
- oversized LLM dependency
- unrealistic world simulation
- generalized AGI ambitions

---

# Guiding Design Principles

## 1. Admissibility Before Intelligence

StegSim is not primarily about making agents smarter.

It is about determining whether:

```text
smart systems remain governable under mutation.
```

Governance geometry takes priority over cognition complexity.

---

## 2. Deterministic Replay First

Every major simulation transition should be:

- replayable
- inspectable
- reconstructable
- attributable

Receipts are first-class primitives.

---

## 3. Mutation Awareness

Most governance systems assume:

- stable policy
- stable trust
- stable topology
- stable incentives

StegSim assumes all of these mutate continuously.

---

## 4. Recoverability Matters More Than Compliance

A compliant system may still become unrecoverable.

StegSim therefore tracks:

- viability margin
- recoverability
- mutation velocity
- legitimacy continuity

instead of policy compliance alone.

# Final StegVerse Thesis

StegSim exists to test one foundational proposition:

> A system may remain compliant while becoming ungovernable.

The purpose of admissibility-aware governance is therefore not merely:

- preventing invalid actions

but preserving:

- recoverability
- legitimacy continuity
- mutation tolerance
- viable future state-space

under continuous environmental and systemic drift.
